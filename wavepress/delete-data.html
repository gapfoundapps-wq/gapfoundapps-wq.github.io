Deleting your data

Wavepress · effective 1 September 2026

SupportPrivacy policyDeleting your data

In short

There is no account to delete, because Wavepress has no accounts. Everything you make is on your phone and nowhere else, so deleting it is something you do on the device, and it is immediate and complete.

We hold nothing to delete on our side. There is no request to send us and no waiting period.

How to delete everything on your phone

  1. Open Wavepress.
  2. Tap the settings icon at the top right of the library.
  3. Under Reset, tap Erase all posters, then confirm with Erase everything.

To delete a single poster instead, open it and use Delete this poster at the bottom of the design screen.

Uninstalling the app does the same thing — it removes the encrypted files and the key that opens them.

This cannot be undone. There is no cloud backup and no copy on our side to restore from. If a poster still matters, export the PDF or PNG and save it somewhere else first.

What we hold, and how to have it deleted

Nothing. Wavepress has no servers, no database and no accounts. No recording, poster, title, date, email address or name ever reaches us, so there is no deletion request to make and nothing we could produce if you asked.

If you want that confirmed in writing, email gapfound.apps@gmail.com and we will confirm it.

What is kept, and for how long

Your purchase

The one-time unlock is recorded against your Apple ID or Google account by Apple or Google, and by RevenueCat against an anonymous identifier generated on your device. That record is what lets Restore purchase work on a new phone, so deleting it would mean losing what you paid for.

It contains no recording, no poster and no personal detail. To have purchase records deleted, contact Apple or Google, who hold them. To have the anonymous RevenueCat identifier deleted, email us and we will request its removal.

Everything else

Nothing else exists to keep.
