In short

Airtight — published by Gap Found.

Airtight stores your records on your own phone and nowhere else. We hold no copy of them, so there is no account for us to close and no database for us to delete you from. You delete your data yourself, in the app, and it is immediate.

Deleting is final. Nobody else holds a copy, so there is nothing for us to restore. Air monitoring and health monitoring records must be kept for 30 years, and training records for 5 years after a worker leaves — those duties stay with your business. Take a backup before you delete anything you still need.

How to delete everything on your phone

  1. Open Airtight.
  2. Go to the Backup screen.
  3. Scroll to “Delete everything on this phone”.
  4. Tap Delete everything.
  5. Type DELETE to confirm, then tap Delete everything permanently.

The app returns to a fresh install. Uninstalling Airtight does the same thing.

What we hold, and how to have it deleted

We hold none of the records above. The only data associated with you anywhere outside your phone is your subscription, and it contains nothing that identifies you.

What is kept, and for how long

By us: nothing at all, once the subscription record above is deleted. There is no account, no backup on our side, and no archive.

On your phone, nothing survives the deletion. Any backup file you exported yourself stays wherever you saved it — we cannot see it or delete it, so delete that separately if you no longer want it.
